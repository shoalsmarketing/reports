Blotato Review: Social Media Automation, Pricing and Trial

Updated August 2026 · Social Media Automation

Blotato is a social media content creation and publishing platform built for creators, small businesses, and agencies. It can turn source material into platform-specific posts, generate visual content, schedule posts, and publish across multiple social networks. It also connects with automation tools such as n8n and Make.

That is the real use case. Blotato is not a project management app, a task tracker, or a general team workspace. Its job is to help you create, repurpose, schedule, and distribute social media content from one system.

Quick verdict: Blotato makes the most sense for people who want content creation and multi-platform publishing in the same workflow. The $29 Starter plan is a practical entry point. Automation users should know that API access is not included during the free trial.

What Is Blotato?

Blotato combines several parts of a social media workflow. You can give it a source, generate drafts for different platforms, add or create visuals, then schedule or publish the finished posts. Sources can include text, articles, YouTube videos, TikTok videos, podcasts, audio files, PDFs, and research prompts.

The platform supports publishing to major networks including Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook Pages, Threads, TikTok, X, Bluesky, YouTube, and Pinterest. The exact post types vary by network, so you should check Blotato's current platform documentation if a particular format is essential to your workflow.

For people building automated content systems, Blotato also offers a REST API, a hosted MCP connection, and official nodes for n8n and Make. That makes it useful as the publishing layer in a larger workflow that may include research, script generation, video creation, and approval steps.

Blotato Features

Multi-Platform Publishing and Scheduling

Blotato lets you connect multiple social accounts and publish from a single dashboard. You can post immediately, schedule content for a specific time, or use recurring time slots. This reduces the copy-and-paste work that comes with managing each platform separately.

The Starter plan currently allows up to 20 connected social accounts. LinkedIn Company Pages and Facebook Pages do not count toward that stated account limit, according to Blotato's support documentation.

Content Repurposing

One of Blotato's main strengths is turning existing material into social posts. Add a video, article, PDF, podcast, or raw text, then generate drafts tailored to the networks you choose. You can edit those drafts before they go live, which is important if you want the content to sound like you instead of generic AI output.

AI Images, Carousels, and Videos

The platform can create images, infographics, carousels, slideshows, and short-form video assets. It also includes brand tools and templates. These features use AI credits, and the number of monthly credits depends on the plan.

Blotato is not a replacement for every specialist video tool. Its own documentation says it does not currently create realistic human avatars or AI clones. A common workflow is to create an avatar video in a platform such as HeyGen and use Blotato to distribute the finished video.

n8n, Make, API, and MCP Connections

Blotato can serve as the final publishing step in an automated content pipeline. Its official n8n and Make nodes are useful for visual automations, while the API and MCP options give developers and AI-agent users more control.

Important trial limitation: The API is available only to paying subscribers. Generating an API key ends the free trial and starts the paid subscription, even if trial days remain.

Blotato Pricing

Blotato currently lists three monthly plans. Pricing and allowances can change, so confirm the final details on the checkout page before subscribing.

Plan Monthly Price Best Fit
Starter $29 Solo creators and small businesses
Creator $97 Higher content volume and more connected accounts
Agency $499 Teams that need larger credit limits and dedicated support

The Starter plan includes the core publishing tools, 20 connected social accounts, AI writing, content sources, n8n and Make nodes, and a monthly allowance of AI credits. Creator increases the account and credit limits. Agency is positioned for teams that need substantially more processing capacity and hands-on support.

Does Blotato Have a Free Trial?

Yes. Blotato advertises a 7-day free trial. The trial includes the main content, scheduling, and publishing features, plus a limited number of credits for AI image and video generation.

The API is the major exception. If your reason for testing Blotato is an n8n, Make, or custom API workflow, build and map the rest of the automation first. Once you generate the Blotato API key, the trial ends and paid billing begins.

Who Is Blotato Best For?

Blotato is a strong fit for creators, solopreneurs, small businesses, and lean agencies that publish frequently across several platforms. It is especially relevant when you already have content worth repurposing or want to connect social publishing to an automated workflow.

It may be more platform than you need if you publish occasionally to one social account. It is also not the right tool if your main requirement is project management, employee task tracking, or long-form video editing.

Blotato FAQ

Is Blotato legit?

Yes. Blotato is an active social media creation and publishing platform with official documentation, direct integrations, and paid plans. As with any publishing automation, start with a small test and review generated content before scheduling at scale.

What social platforms does Blotato support?

Blotato supports Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook Pages, Threads, TikTok, X, Bluesky, YouTube, and Pinterest. Available formats and platform limits differ.

Can Blotato work with n8n?

Yes. Blotato provides an official n8n node and API documentation. API access requires a paid subscription.

Does Blotato offer a coupon code?

No public Blotato coupon code is confirmed here. The standard offer is a 7-day free trial. Check the current checkout page for any active promotion.

Can Blotato make AI avatar videos?

Blotato can generate several types of social media visuals and videos, but its documentation says it does not create realistic human avatars or AI clones. You can create those videos elsewhere and publish them through Blotato.

Final Verdict

Blotato is best understood as a content repurposing and social publishing system with automation-friendly connections. The value is not just writing a caption. It is moving from source material to edited posts, visuals, scheduling, and multi-platform distribution without stitching together as many separate tools.

The 7-day trial is enough to evaluate the dashboard, content generation, and normal publishing workflow. If API automation is the deciding factor, remember that activating the API starts the paid plan.
