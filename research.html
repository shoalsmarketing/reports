Research

Shoals Marketing reviews software from the perspective of a buyer trying to understand the product, the offer, and the practical tradeoffs before signing up.

Sources and verification

Research may include firsthand product access, official product websites, pricing and checkout pages, help documentation, release notes, support materials, and publicly available product demonstrations.

Firsthand testing is not claimed unless it occurred. When a conclusion is based on published documentation rather than direct access, the review is written accordingly.

What is checked

  • The product's primary purpose and intended users
  • Important features and meaningful limitations
  • Current plan structure and published pricing
  • Trial length, payment requirements, and trial restrictions
  • Integrations, supported platforms, and usage limits
  • Whether a discount or coupon is publicly available

Pricing and changing offers

Prices, plan names, included features, and promotional terms can change without notice. Reviews include an update date when material details have been checked. Readers should confirm the final terms on the provider's website before purchasing.

Ratings and recommendations

A recommendation considers product fit, usability, feature depth, limitations, pricing, and the type of buyer the product serves. A product can be a strong choice for one workflow and a poor choice for another.

Commercial relationships

Some product links may result in compensation to Shoals Marketing. Commercial relationships do not change the product facts, limitations, or trial restrictions included in a review.

Corrections

Product information changes quickly. If a page contains an outdated price, discontinued feature, broken link, or factual error, send the page URL and the corrected information through the Contact page.
