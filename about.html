About Shoals Marketing

Shoals Marketing publishes reviews, pricing breakdowns, trial guides, and buyer resources for digital marketing software.

Coverage includes marketing platforms, webinar software, automation tools, content creation products, and services used to build and promote online businesses.

What the site is built to do

Software pages often make products sound interchangeable. Shoals Marketing focuses on the details that affect a buying decision: what a product does, who it fits, what it costs, which limitations matter, and what a trial actually includes.

Reviews are written for readers comparing options or checking an offer before purchasing. Product terms can change, so dated information is reviewed and corrected as new details become available.

How coverage is selected

Priority is given to products connected to digital marketing, automation, content production, lead generation, sales funnels, and online training.

For details about sources, product access, pricing checks, and corrections, read the Research page.
